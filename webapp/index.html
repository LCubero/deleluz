Help

Fix a problem

  1. Close the LED LAMP app. Only one phone or app can connect at a time.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth and Location.
  3. Stand within a few meters of the strip and tap Connect.
  1. Move closer and check that the controller has steady power.
  2. Close other apps that control the strip, then tap Reconnect.

  2. Turn it on in Home, raise brightness, and try White. The page can't read the strip's actual state.
